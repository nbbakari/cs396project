Impact factors{% if factors %} · EPA TRACI {{ factors[0].version }}{% endif %}

EPA's TRACI characterization factors (how strongly 1 kg of each pollutant adds to an impact) for the pollutants CAMPD reports, emitted to air. A potential impact is the emission in kilograms (1 short ton = 907.18474 kg) times its factor, added up only within one impact category. Units such as “kg SO2 eq” (equivalent) mean the amount of the reference pollutant that would have the same effect. These are potential impacts for comparison, not measured harm.
