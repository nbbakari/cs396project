What validation checks

Rejected (not imported, listed in the report):

  • blank or unreadable facility ID, unit ID or year;
  • a year before 1995 (when CAMPD reporting began) or in the future;
  • a non-numeric or negative operating time, load, heat input or emission mass;
  • operating time above 8,784 hours (a leap year);
  • the same facility, unit and year twice in the file (the later row is kept);
  • a unit-year already in the database (the stored record is kept).

Imported with a warning:

  • a blank required metric, recorded as 0 (a unit that did not run);
  • an invalid state code, coordinate or date, left blank;
  • text longer than its database column, truncated.
