Compare and rank

Compare facilities, states, fuels, unit types or years on one variable. Each group gets a rank (1 = highest), how much higher or lower it is than a reference (in the variable's own unit and as a percentage), and where it stands among the other groups. A statistical test then says whether the differences are bigger than chance alone would produce.
