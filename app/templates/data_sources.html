EPA Clean Air Markets Program Data (CAMPD)

Emissions, operating and compliance data for regulated power plants, published by the U.S. EPA.

  • Access: CAMPD API and bulk data downloads (API key held server-side only)
  • Granularity used: annual, unit-level records
  • Measures: operating time, gross/steam load, heat input, CO₂, SO₂ and NOₓ mass, control equipment
